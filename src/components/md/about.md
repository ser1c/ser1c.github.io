I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at the [University of Strathclyde](https://www.strath.ac.uk/business/economics/), submitting in September 2026. I use quasi-experimental methods to study health insurance and health financing in low- and middle-income countries, working with around 47 million administrative claims records from Nepal's National Health Insurance Programme.

**Research Interests:** Health Economics · Health Insurance and Financing · Applied Microeconomics · Development Economics
